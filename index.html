About & common questions
How did this start?

In our community, the same questions come up every year. When does Great Lent start? When is Coptic Easter? When does the Nativity Fast begin? What day is the feast of Archangel Michael, St. Mary or St. George? The answers are usually spread across church bulletins, apps and printed calendars, and they're easy to miss.

So we built a simple way to put the Coptic calendar where people already look every day: their own phone calendar. Pick the saints and the feasts and fasts that matter to you, add them to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar once, and they stay up to date, year after year.

Fast and feast dates

When does Great Lent start this year?

Great Lent begins on a Monday, 55 days before Coptic Easter, including Holy Week.

When is Coptic Easter (the Feast of the Resurrection)?

Coptic Easter follows the Alexandrian calculation, so it often falls later than Western Easter.

When does the Nativity Fast start?

The Nativity Fast begins on Hathor 16 and lasts 43 days, until Coptic Christmas.

When is Coptic Christmas?

The Coptic Orthodox Church celebrates the Nativity on January 7.

When is the Fast of Nineveh (Jonah's Fast)?

The Fast of Nineveh is three days, Monday to Wednesday, two weeks before Great Lent.

When is the Apostles' Fast?

The Apostles' Fast begins the day after Pentecost and ends on Epip 4, the eve of the Feast of the Apostles.

When is the Fast of St. Mary?

The Fast of St. Mary runs from Mesori 1 to Mesori 15 (August 7–21), ending with the Feast of the Assumption.

What is today's Coptic date?

The Coptic calendar has 12 months of 30 days and a short 13th month called Nasie.

Using the calendar

Does it work with iPhone and Google Calendar?

Yes. Tap Add to Apple Calendar on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, or Add to Google Calendar for Google (works best from a computer). It also works with any calendar app that can subscribe to a calendar link, like Outlook.

Will my calendar update when the dates change each year?

Yes. Movable feasts and fasts like Great Lent, Coptic Easter and the Apostles' Fast are worked out every year, and your calendar always shows the next two years ahead. You never need to add it again.

What is the Joyous 29th?

On the 29th of each Coptic month, the Church commemorates the Annunciation, the Nativity and the Resurrection together. It isn't observed in the months of Tobi and Meshir.

Why does the reminder come the night before?

In the Coptic Church, the day begins at sunset, so a feast is celebrated starting the evening before, beginning with Vespers. The reminder lets you know the night it begins.
